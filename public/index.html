Frequently Asked Questions / FAQ

How to resize an image for phone or desktop wallpaper without cropping?

Upload your picture to studiowallpaper, select your target screen resolution, and choose either blurred pixel padding or a solid color background. The original photo remains 100% visible in full ratio.

Is my uploaded image sent to any server?

No. All calculations and image modifications are executed locally inside your web browser via HTML5 Canvas API. Your files never leave your device.
